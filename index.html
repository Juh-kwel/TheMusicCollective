A Tuskegee sound

A collective sound.
A whole lot of soul.

The music Collective brings together Tuskegee musicians with one thing in common: a love for music that brings people together. With R&B at the heart of what we do, we blend smooth vocals, rich harmonies, and live-band energy into a sound made for singing along, moving, and making memories.

Feel-good grooves, expressive vocals, and the warmth of a live band. Our sound is grounded in R&B, with neo-soul textures and the musicianship of our jazz roots.

Tuskegee to statewide

Rooted here. Ready to travel.

We’re current Tuskegee University students, based in Tuskegee, Alabama. Our regional performances have taken us to Montgomery and Birmingham, and we’re glad to bring our sound to events across the state.

We’re an independent student group, not an official Tuskegee University organization. Campus community members and outside clients are all welcome to book us. If we need to travel further, we can be contacted, and there is a section for it on the request page.
